Little Mushroom

My biggest interest right now is the comic and novel series Little Mushroom, written by Yi Shi Si Zhou. It follows the story of a mushroom who pretends to be a human in order to infiltrate a human base to get back his spore that was taken from him. He's unlike the other monsters who pretend to be human - called xenogenics - though, as he is does not intend to kill any humans in the process of his mission.

Games

As expected from a game developer, I enjoy playing a variety of games. No matter how well I am or am not at playing them.

My favorite kinds of games are gacha games, but I also lean towards chill games that you are able to take at your own pace. I dislike games with time limits or require you to have fast reactions, or good hand-eye-coordination, as these are things I lack.

Coding

I really enjoy coding! Though it may be difficult, I enjoy the process of starting from nothing and coding from scratch something that could help myself or help someone else. Or even a program with very little function at all.

My dream is to be able to develop a game that I am proud of, that other people also like.
